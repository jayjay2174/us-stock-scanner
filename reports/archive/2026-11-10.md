## Methodology and limitations

The price/momentum score combines cross-sectional robust z-scores for 1-day, 5-day and 20-day returns, relative volume, distance above the 20-day average and RSI. The final score weights price/momentum at 85% and a simple headline-keyword signal at 15%. Headline tone is a triage aid, not full sentiment analysis.

The live universe comes from Nasdaq Trader symbol directories. Adjusted daily prices, market capitalization and headlines come from Yahoo Finance through yfinance. These free sources can be delayed, rate-limited, incomplete or subject to terms-of-use changes. Unknown market caps are excluded.
